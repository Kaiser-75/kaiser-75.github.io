We introduce CoRE, a weakly supervised framework for learning human-perceived risk in egocentric driving videos by transforming clip-level subjective risk ratings into prediction-effect supervision for perceived-risk scoring, temporal risk-support localization, and tracked-object contribution estimation.

<sup>†</sup> Equal supervision.
